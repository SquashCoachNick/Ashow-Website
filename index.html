About the club

Centuries of stories, and more to come

Ashow Village Club and Reading Room is an 18th-century, Grade II listed building on the village lane, once part of the Stoneleigh estate. Today it's a members' club run by a volunteer committee elected at the AGM.

Hire the club

Members can hire the club for birthdays, anniversaries and family gatherings for themselves and their guests.

Email hire@ashowvillageclub.co.uk with your date and numbers, and a committee member will get back to you.
