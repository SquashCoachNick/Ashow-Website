Privacy notice

Your information, looked after

Ashow Village Club is run by volunteers. We only keep the information we need to run the club, we never sell it, and we don't use it for marketing.

In short

  • This website doesn't use cookies, tracking or analytics.
  • We hold members' contact details so we can run the club and keep in touch about it.
  • When you email or book an event, we use your details only for that.
  • You can ask to see, correct or delete what we hold about you at any time: email hello@ashowvillageclub.co.uk.

Who we are

Ashow Village Club and Reading Room ("the club", "we") is a private members' club in Ashow, Kenilworth, Warwickshire CV8 2LE, run by a volunteer committee elected at the AGM. The club is responsible for the personal information described here. For anything about privacy, email hello@ashowvillageclub.co.uk and a committee member will reply.

Our lawful reasons

  • Membership and bookings: it's necessary to provide what you've asked for (your membership, a place at an event, or a hire).
  • Club records and accounts: to meet our legal obligations, for example keeping financial records.
  • Keeping members informed: our legitimate interest in running the club and telling members about club business and events. You can ask us to stop at any time.
  • Photos: your consent, which you can withdraw at any time.

This website

The website doesn't set cookies, and we don't use analytics or advertising tools. It does rely on a few outside services, which will see your device's IP address when you visit:

  • GitHub Pages hosts the website and keeps short-term server logs, including IP addresses, for security.
  • Google Fonts supplies the lettering used on the site.
  • Google Calendar supplies the list of events. The event details are ones we publish ourselves; your browser fetches them directly from Google.

The "Add to calendar" buttons only contact Google or Microsoft if you choose one of them. The Apple and other option creates the calendar file on your own device.

Who else sees your information

Only committee members who need it for their role, plus the services we use to run the club:

  • Google (email, calendar, forms and documents for club records)
  • ImprovMX (forwards emails sent to hello@ashowvillageclub.co.uk to the club's email account)
  • PayPal / Zettle and our bank (payments)
  • WhatsApp, if you join a club WhatsApp group or reply to book an event: other members of that group can see your name and phone number

Some of these providers store information outside the UK, mainly in the United States. They do so under recognised safeguards, such as the UK-US data bridge or standard contractual clauses. We never sell or share your information for marketing, and we only pass it to anyone else if the law requires it.

Your rights

You can ask us to:

  • give you a copy of the information we hold about you
  • correct anything that's wrong or out of date
  • delete your information, where we don't need to keep it
  • stop or limit how we use it, or object to our using it
  • give you your information in a form you can take elsewhere

Email hello@ashowvillageclub.co.uk and we'll reply within one month. There's no charge.

If you're unhappy with how we've handled your information, please tell us first and we'll try to put it right. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint or on 0303 123 1113.
